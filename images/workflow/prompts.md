# Original workflow illustrations

Created for Yadunand Pavithran’s portfolio on 2026-11-06 using the built-in ImageGen tool.

These are newly generated illustrations. No Code and Thrive artwork, logos, screenshots, or images were supplied as visual references. The Discover image establishes the series; the other four use that newly generated image only as a reference for materials, palette, and lighting.

Final website assets: `discover.webp`, `design.webp`, `develop.webp`, `deploy.webp`, and `refine.webp`. Encoded from the generated PNG originals as WebP at their original dimensions for website delivery. The original PNGs remain in the generation tool’s output folder; the site references only the assets in this directory.

## Final prompts

### Discover

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one original premium editorial 3D still-life image for the Discover stage of an independent software engineer and product designer's portfolio. This is a new artwork, not a redesign or imitation of any existing company's imagery. Portrait 4:5 composition. A tactile off-white tabletop and warm-white backdrop, with a sculptural investigation board made of several thick ivory paper cards and thin frosted acrylic sheets arranged in a loose branching map. A single burnt-orange thread (#d63f1c) finds a clear route through small charcoal pins, converging at a small orange sphere seen through a large elegant charcoal circular viewfinder standing in the middle. This should suggest curiosity, research, and defining a digital product, not architecture. Art direction: restrained museum still-life photography, analog materials, matte graphite, soft white paper edges, subtle grain, natural side light, credible soft shadows, sophisticated and quiet. Palette strictly off-white, near-black #111111, soft gray, and one restrained burnt-orange accent. Central composition with generous breathing room; all essential objects inside the central 60% so this also crops well to landscape on phones. No text, letters, numbers, logos, company names, watermarks, device screens, branded interfaces, books, or marketing cards. One continuous scene, not a collage, not a grid, not a mockup presentation.
