Nine houses, stepped so that every roof is a neighbour's garden and no one looks into anyone else's rooms.

We kept every almond tree we could and laid the houses out around them, which is why no two plans are quite the same.

Three houses remain. Each comes with its own well, a shaded court and a roof garden planted by the orchard's previous owner.
