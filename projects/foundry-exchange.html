A workplace should give something back at street level. Foundry Exchange gives the neighbourhood a yard, a canteen and a short cut.

We kept the foundry's plot lines and its hard brick, then opened the block with a passage that lines up with the old tram route. The ground floor is let at affordable rents to the workshops that were here before.

Floors are deep enough to be reorganised and tall enough to outlive offices altogether. The structure is designed so it could become housing in thirty years without being rebuilt.
