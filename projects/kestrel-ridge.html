Kestrel Ridge House

Category
Residential
Status

Location
Douro Valley, PT
Year

Client
Private
Area
410 m²

We dug the house into the old terrace so that from the road you see a wall, a line of light and nothing else.

The site came with a dry stone terrace that had held the hillside for two centuries. Rather than build on top of it, we rebuilt the wall forty metres along and set the rooms behind it, so the house takes its line from the land.

Inside, a single corridor runs the full length of the plan with the rooms opening off it to the south. Board-marked concrete carries the roof; everything you touch is oak, lime plaster or brass.
