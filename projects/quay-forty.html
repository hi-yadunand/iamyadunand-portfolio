When we develop as well as design, we can make the decisions that usually get value-engineered away. Quay Forty is the proof.

We bought the quay in 2021 and spent two years on the ground floor before we drew a single apartment. It holds a market hall, a rowing club and a public stair to the water.

A third of the homes are let at below-market rents and managed by a local housing association. The rest are for sale, with every plan drawn to take a family, a couple or a home office.
