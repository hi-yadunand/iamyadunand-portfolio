A–07How I work

05 stages

From an idea
to launch.

01
02
03
04
05

  1. 01

    Discover

    I start by understanding your goals, audience, and what your product needs to do. Together, we define the scope, priorities, and experience we want to create.

  2. 02

    Design

    I turn the brief into user flows, wireframes, and prototypes. Layout, typography, and visual identity come together before I move into development.

  3. 03

    Develop

    I build responsive websites and web applications with reusable interfaces and the integrations they need. Each interaction is shaped around how people will use the product.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    I test the experience across screen sizes, check the details, and prepare it for launch. Performance, accessibility, and SEO foundations are part of the process.

  5. 05

    Refine & Repeat

    After launch, I use feedback to improve the experience and plan the next iteration. I keep refining the product as new needs and ideas take shape.
