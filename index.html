A–07How we work

05 stages

From a pencil to the keys

01
02
03
04
05

  1. 01

    Listen

    A week on the site and in your life, with no computers. We end it with one sheet of paper and an honest view on whether to build at all.

  2. 02

    Model

    We make everything in card and timber first. Clients, neighbours and planners can all understand a model on a table.

  3. 03

    Detail

    Every junction drawn at 1:5 with the people who will make it. Materials are tested on site for a full year when we can manage it.

  4. 04

    Build

    We stay on site through to handover, every fortnight at minimum. No one takes over our drawings from us part-way through.

  5. 05

    Return

    We come back at one, five and ten years to see what has worn well. What we learn goes into the next building.
